# 🖼️ Album Express — E-Commerce Platform for Photo Albums

A **modern and responsive e-commerce website** built for **Album Express**, a company specializing in **photo album sales**.  
The project focuses on **speed, usability, and visual appeal**, providing users with an intuitive and elegant online shopping experience.

🔗 **Live Website:** [https://albumexpress.mx](https://albumexpress.mx)  
💻 **Source Code:** [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/Adaro45/AlbumExpress/)

---

## 🌐 Project Overview

The **Album Express** platform was designed to deliver a fast and frictionless user experience while maintaining a clean and modern aesthetic.  
Users can browse through categorized products, search efficiently, and easily contact the store through integrated forms.

The focus of development was on:
- **Performance optimization**  
- **Responsive design** for all devices  
- **Intuitive UX/UI** with smooth animations  
- **Scalability** for future product expansion

## ⚙️ Technical Challenges & Solutions

| Challenge | Solution |
|------------|-----------|
| **Maintaining high performance with many assets** | Implemented **lazy loading** and **code splitting** to minimize bundle size |
| **Designing complex UX/UI layouts** | Used **modular CSS** and component-driven design to ensure flexibility and consistency |
| **Smooth transitions and animations** | Integrated **CSS transitions** and **React motion effects** for natural flow between components |
